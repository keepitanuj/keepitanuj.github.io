the 2am grocery store is the last third place

nobody is performing anything at 2am. you're just a guy holding oat milk under fluorescent light, and so is everyone else.

i've started going on purpose. not for anything in particular. it's the only place i know where strangers are in the same room, doing the same small thing, and nobody needs anything from anybody.

the cashier and i have a nod now. it took four months.

i don't know her name. i don't want to know her name. the nod is perfect and i won't ruin it.

no cookies, no tracking, no newsletter popup. made by hand, slowly.
