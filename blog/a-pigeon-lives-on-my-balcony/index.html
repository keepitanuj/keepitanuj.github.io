notes on the pigeon that lives on my balcony

his name is kevin. he did not choose it and does not answer to it.

he arrived in june. he sits on the railing from roughly sunrise to roughly whenever, looking at the building across the road like he's waiting for it to apologise.

  • he does not like bread. he likes the idea of bread.
  • he has a friend who visits on weekends. i assume it's his friend. it could be his landlord.
  • he has seen me cry once and was very normal about it.
