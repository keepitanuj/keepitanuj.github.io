every bench in my neighborhood, ranked

eleven benches. four criteria: comfort, view, shade, and what i'm calling "vibe integrity," which is whether the bench feels like it wants you there.

11the one outside the bank

metal. armrests every forty centimetres so you can't lie down. a bench that has been designed by someone who hates benches. it should be arrested.

06the bus stop one

it's fine. it's a bench. it knows what it is and it doesn't pretend to be anything else, which honestly is more than i can say for most people.

01the one by the old tree

wooden, slightly warped, one plank missing. full shade until 3pm. a man feeds birds there every thursday and has never once acknowledged me, which is exactly the right amount of acknowledgement.
