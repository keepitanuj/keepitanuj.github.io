i'm anuj. i notice things and then can't let them go.

right now i'm a student / working as something in city. most of my day is spent on the main thing, and most of my evenings are spent on everything else.

this site is where the everything else goes. it isn't a portfolio and it isn't a brand. it's closer to a notebook that i'm okay with you reading: rankings nobody asked for, small experiments on myself, thoughts that are too long for a caption and too dumb for an essay.

01things i'm into

a few honest interests, e.g. films, a sport, a specific kind of music, cooking one dish very well.

02things i'm bad at

replying to texts on time. leaving parties. knowing when a post is finished.

03how this site works

everything on the front page is laid out by time. the space between two posts is the time between them. long gaps stay long. i'd rather you see that i disappeared for two months than pretend i didn't.

the now page works the same way: older updates fade until they're barely there.

04say hi

email is best: hi@…. i read everything. i reply to most of it, eventually.

no cookies, no tracking, no newsletter popup. made by hand, slowly.
